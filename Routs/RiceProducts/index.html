How we process your rice flakes

A seamless four-step commercial workflow — from raw paddy cleaning and precision steaming to roller flattening and hygienic moisture-barrier packaging.

01

Paddy Cleaning & Grading

Raw paddy grains are carefully filtered and cleaned to remove impurities, selecting only the finest raw rice grains.

02

Steaming & Roasting

Rice grains undergo controlled hydrothermal steaming and roasting to soften the kernel structure for ideal flaking.

03

Roller Flattening

Advanced heavy-duty rollers flatten the conditioned rice grains smoothly into crisp, uniform white rice flakes (poha).

04

Hygienic Packaging

Freshly made crispy rice flakes are sorted, graded, and sealed in airtight moisture-proof packs to retain natural taste.
